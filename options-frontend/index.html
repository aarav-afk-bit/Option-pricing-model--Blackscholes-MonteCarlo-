    Convergence: the estimate settles as paths are added
    Simulated futures: where the price could go
    Black-Scholes is a formula: one constant volatility, smooth bell-curve returns. Monte Carlo prices the option by simulating thousands of possible futures and averaging the payoff — which lets the lab drop those assumptions. With constant volatility both must agree; the bracket shows the simulation's 95% uncertainty.
